# HANDOFF: INDOTERM Linear Solver (Stage 2a - Schur/Condensation)
**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Status:** Stage 2a (Linear Algebra Seam) implemented and verified.

## 🎯 Goal
Allow the INDOTERM model to scale from 6 to 34 regions without hitting the LU memory wall (Projected: ~57M nnz vs 250M+ LU).

## 🛠️ What has been implemented
1. **Schur Complement Solver:** `src/schur_linsolve.jl` now provides a fast, direct alternative to `lu(A)`.
2. **Numerical Stability:** Introduced `SCHUR_DFLOOR = 1e-3` to prevent "dust pivot" amplification on ill-conditioned scenarios (e.g., `coalprice`).
3. **The "Seam":** `src/solve_newton!.jl` and `src/arclength.jl` now support `linsolve = :schur`.
4. **Bordered Solve:** `schur_bordered` allows the pseudo-arclength corrector to use Schur solves instead of full LU factorizations for the augmented system.

## ✅ Verification Results
- **Seam Equivalence:** `_probe_seam.jl` verified that `linsolve = :schur` produces results bit-identical to LU (max|Δ| $\approx 3.1\text{e-}13$) on the `coalprice` scenario.
- **Path Independence:** `_probe_seam_paths.jl` confirmed that changing step sizes (h=1 vs h=0.05) under `:schur` yields consistent results ($\approx 8.3\text{e-}7$), matching the LU path.
- **Regression:** `verify_arclength_fallback.jl` passed, confirming that the arclength plumbing remains intact after the refactor.
